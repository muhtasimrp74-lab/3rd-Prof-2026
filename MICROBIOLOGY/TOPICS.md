# Microbiology — BMDC Syllabus

Source: BMDC MBBS Curriculum 2021, Microbiology (https://www.bmdc.org.bd/docs/curriculum/2021/12.Microbiology.pdf)
Exam: 15 Nov — Written 100 (MCQ 20 [MTF 10 + SBA 10] + SAQ/SEQ 70 + Formative 10), SOE 100, Practical 100.

Status key: 🟢 Strong | 🟡 Needs revision | 🔴 Weak | ⚪ Not studied

Notes:
- Core = BMDC "CORE". Additional = BMDC "Additional".
- BMDC item cards: Card 1 = General Bacteriology, Parasitology, Immunology (+ Molecular Biology). Card 2 = Systemic Bacteriology, Virology, Mycology, Clinical Microbiology.
- BMDC hours (Lecture / Tutorial / Practical): General Bact 13/7/15, Systemic Bact 20/15/15, Immunology 16/4/1, Parasitology 17/8/6, Virology 14/4/1, Mycology 6/2/1, Clinical Micro 14/5/6.

# GENERAL BACTERIOLOGY

### MICRO-GB-01 Introduction of Microbiology
Status: ⚪
- Brief historical background
- Branches of Microbiology
- Legends in the field of Microbiology
- Koch's postulate, molecular Koch's postulate, the limitations and new adjuncts
- Concept of medical biotechnology in relation to Microbiology
- Importance and scope of microbiology in medical science

### MICRO-GB-02 Bacterial cell
Status: ⚪
- Prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells with examples
- Different structures of bacterial cell and their functions
- Brief description of cell wall of Gram positive and Gram negative bacteria
- Spores: structure and clinical importance
- L-forms, protoplast, spheroplast; clinical importance of L-form

### MICRO-GB-03 Bacterial classification and staining
Status: ⚪
- Nomenclature of bacteria
- Classification by staining, morphology, oxygen requirement, temperature requirement
- Staining: theoretical basis and clinical significance of Gram and Z-N stain, Albert stain, Auramine-Rhodamine stain
- Practical on staining: Gram, Z-N, Albert

### MICRO-GB-04 Nutrition and cultivation of bacteria
Status: ⚪
- Nutritional requirement for the growth of bacteria
- Growth curve: phases with clinical significance
- Common bacteriological media: classification and uses

### MICRO-GB-05 Sterilization and disinfection
Status: ⚪
- Definition, classification and applications of sterilization, disinfection and antisepsis
- Methods of sterilization: details of autoclaving, hot air oven and chemical methods
- Sterilization of medical equipment and culture media
- Disinfection of body fluid spillage and equipment
- Preparation of disinfectants and their use

### MICRO-GB-06 Antimicrobial agents
Status: ⚪
- Definition of antibiotics, antimicrobial agents, chemotherapeutics, bacteriostatic, bactericidal, synergism, antagonism, selective toxicity etc.
- Classification of antimicrobial agents
- Mechanism of action on bacteria with examples
- Drug resistance: origin, mechanism, transmission and prevention
- Indication of combination of antibiotics in bacterial infection
- Hazards of indiscriminate use of antibiotics
- Defining MDR, XDR and PDR bacteria
- Definition and importance of ESBL, MBL, MRSA, VRSA, VRE
- Definition and importance of biofilm

### MICRO-GB-07 Host-parasite relationship
Status: ⚪
- Terms and definitions
- Parasite and host attributes
- Normal flora, opportunistic pathogens and their clinical importance

### MICRO-GB-08 Pathogenesis of bacterial diseases
Status: ⚪
- Transmission of bacterial agents
- Virulence factors (antigens, toxins, enzymes, invasiveness) and their role in pathogenesis, with examples

### MICRO-GB-09 Bacterial genetics
Status: ⚪
- Bacterial genome, DNA, chromosome, plasmid, transposon etc.
- Gene transfer in bacteria
- Bacterial DNA replication
- DNA recombination, principles of cloning and genetic engineering

### MICRO-GB-10 Septic shock
Status: ⚪
- Listed by BMDC under General Bacteriology (no sub-points)

# BMDC Item Card Topics (cross-reference)

General Bacteriology (1st assessment)
1. Prokaryote and eukaryote, components of bacteria, cell wall of Gram positive and negative bacteria, capsule, flagella, spore, classification of bacteria
2. Growth and death of bacteria, growth requirements, classification according to oxygen requirement, growth curve, generation time
3. Pathogenesis of bacterial disease, exotoxin and endotoxin, Koch's postulates, limitations, new adjuncts, molecular Koch's postulates; host defense against bacterial disease
4. Sterilization, disinfection, antisepsis, different methods, principles and uses
5. Practical bacteriology: use of microscope, Gram staining, Ziehl-Neelsen staining; culture media — types, commonly used media with use
6. Antimicrobial drugs, mechanism of action, resistance, selective toxicity, antibiotic combination, chemoprophylaxis, susceptibility test; bacterial genetics — plasmid, transposons, mutation, gene transfer; MDR, XDR, PDR bacteria
7. Normal flora
8. Biosafety and biosecurity, biomedical waste disposal

Immunology (1st assessment)
1. Immunity, its type, components of innate immunity, active vs passive immunity, immunocompetent cells
2. Immunogen, antigen, properties of an ideal antigen, hapten
3. Immunoglobulin, antibody, structure, types, function
4. Complements, MHC
5. Cytokines, mechanism of immune response, primary and secondary immune response
6. Tolerance, hypersensitivity, autoimmune diseases
7. Tumour immunity, transplantation, immunodeficiency
8. Immunological reactions: basic principles and examples

Molecular Biology (1st assessment)
1. Principle of PCR, RT-PCR, real-time PCR
2. DNA cloning, DNA recombination, genetic engineering, biotechnology, gene therapy

Parasitology (1st assessment)
1. Basic concepts of host, parasites and their types; classification of medically important protozoa
2. Entamoeba, free living amoeba, Giardia, Balantidium
3. Leishmania, Trichomonas, Trypanosoma
4. Plasmodium, Toxoplasma, Babesia
5. Basic structure and classification of helminths; Cestode: Taenia, Echinococcus, Diphyllobothrium; Trematodes: Schistosoma, Fasciolopsis
6. Nematodes: Ascaris, Enterobius, Strongyloides, Trichuris
7. Nematodes: Hookworm, Filariasis, Onchocerca volvulus

Mycology (2nd assessment)
1. Basic structure of fungi, classification, antifungal drugs
2. Superficial and cutaneous fungi: Malassezia furfur, dermatophytes, Candida
3. Subcutaneous, deep and opportunistic fungi: Madurella, Rhinosporidium, Cryptococcus, Aspergillus

Virology (2nd assessment)
1. Basic virology, basic structure of a virus, defective virus, prion, replication, pathogenesis of viral disease, host defense against viral infection, antiviral drugs, general scheme of lab diagnosis, common viral infections in Bangladesh
2. Herpesvirus, orthomyxovirus, paramyxovirus, rubella virus
3. Hepatitis viruses, oncogenic viruses
4. HIV
5. Polio virus, rabies virus, dengue virus, rotavirus, chikungunya virus, Zika virus
6. COVID-19

Systemic Bacteriology (2nd assessment)
1. Staphylococcus
2. Streptococcus
3. Neisseria, causes of pyogenic meningitis
4. Corynebacterium, Bacillus
5. Mycobacterium
6. Enterobacteriaceae: general properties and classification, Escherichia coli, Shigella
7. Salmonella
8. Vibrio, Campylobacter
9. Pseudomonas, Proteus, Klebsiella
10. Haemophilus, Helicobacter, Bordetella, Bacillus
11. Anaerobic bacteria, anaerobic culture
12. Spirochaetes, sexually transmitted disease
13. Rickettsia, Chlamydia, Mycoplasma

Clinical Microbiology (2nd assessment)
1. Examination of stool, morphology of common parasites in stool, diarrhea — causes and diagnosis
2. Examination of urine, UTI — causes and diagnosis
3. Examination of CSF, meningitis — causes and diagnosis
4. Blood culture, pyrexia of unknown origin
5. Examination of sputum, throat swab, pus, wound swab, pleural fluid, ascitic fluid, genital specimen; causes of pneumonia, sore throat, wound infection, pleural effusion, ascites, vaginal discharge, urethral discharge
6. Basics of hospital acquired infection
7. Infection prevention and control, hand washing, donning and doffing, preparation of disinfectants, disposal of medical wastes
