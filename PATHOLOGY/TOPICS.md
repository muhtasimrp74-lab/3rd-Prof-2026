# Pathology — BMDC Syllabus

Source: BMDC MBBS Curriculum 2021, Pathology (https://www.bmdc.org.bd/docs/curriculum/2021/11.Pathology.pdf)
Exam: 12 Nov — Written 100 (MCQ 20 + SAQ/SEQ 70 + Formative 10), SOE 100, Practical/OSPE 100.

Status key: 🟢 Strong | 🟡 Needs revision | 🔴 Weak | ⚪ Not studied

Notes:
- "Class nos." (L / T / P) are BMDC lecture / tutorial / practical session numbers, not hours.
- Core = must know. Additional = extra depth.
- Term I = General Pathology + Haematolymphoid (1A, 1B). Term II = Systemic Pathology (2A, 2B).
- Clinical pathology items are taught inside systemic chapters: urine exam + KFT (Renal), semen analysis (Male genital), LFT (Hepatobiliary), CSF (CNS), body fluids (Edema chapter).

# TERM 1A — General Pathology

### PATH-1A-01 Introduction to pathology
Status: ⚪ | Class nos.: L 1, T 1, P 0
Core:
- Introduction to different branches of pathology
- Definition of aetiology, morphology and pathogenesis

### PATH-1A-02 Cell injury
Status: ⚪ | Class nos.: L 2,3,4; T 2,3; P 0
Core:
- Cause of cell injury
- Reversible and irreversible injury: mechanism
- Mechanism of hypoxic injury
- Name of free radical, target of free radical and scavenging system (name of the anti-oxidant), definition of reperfusion injury
- Definition of necrosis and apoptosis, types of necrosis and morphologic feature with examples
Additional:
- Mechanism of free radical injury and reperfusion injury, apoptosis
- Consequences of mitochondrial dysfunction and loss of calcium homeostasis

### PATH-1A-03 Pigments and calcification
Status: ⚪ | Class nos.: L 5; T 3
Core:
- Pathological calcification: dystrophic and metastatic — definitions with examples
- Different intracellular pigmentation, particularly their names
Additional:
- Mechanism of calcification

### PATH-1A-04 Acute inflammation
Status: ⚪ | Class nos.: L 6,7,8,9; T 4,5; P 1
Core:
- Causes and cardinal signs or features of acute inflammation
- Vascular and cellular events; chemical mediators and their function
- Morphological patterns of acute inflammation
- Outcome of acute inflammation
- Local and systemic effect of acute inflammation
Additional:
- Recruitment of leukocytes
- Role of complement, coagulation and kinin system
- Mechanism of neutrophil recruitment
- Recognition of microbes and dead tissue
- Defects in leukocyte function
- How the chemical mediator works

### PATH-1A-05 Chronic inflammation
Status: ⚪ | Class nos.: L 10; T 6; P 2
Core:
- Cause
- Difference with acute inflammation
- Role of macrophage
- Examples of granulomatous lesion
- Type of granuloma
- Mechanism of granuloma
Additional:
- Giant cells

### PATH-1A-06 Repair and healing
Status: ⚪ | Class nos.: L 11,12; T 7
Core:
- Definition of healing, repair and regeneration
- Steps of cutaneous wound healing
- Factors influencing wound healing
- Complications of wound healing
- Fracture healing
- Nerve regeneration
Additional:
- Stem cell
- Growth cycle
- Extracellular matrix

### PATH-1A-07 Edema and electrolyte disorder
Status: ⚪ | Class nos.: L 13,14; T 8
Core:
- Pathophysiology of oedema
- Mechanism of oedema in cirrhosis, renal disease and heart failure
- Examination of body fluids such as pleural effusion, ascitic fluid
- Electrolyte disorder: causes of metabolic acidosis, metabolic alkalosis, respiratory acidosis and respiratory alkalosis

### PATH-1A-08 Hyperemia, congestion and haemorrhage and Shock
Status: ⚪ | Class nos.: L 15,16; T 9,10; P 3
Core:
- Definition of hyperaemia, congestion and haemorrhage
- Cause of passive congestion in lung and liver
- Shock: type, pathogenesis of septic shock, stages
Additional:
- Morphology of passive congestion in lung and liver
- Mechanism of compensation in shock

### PATH-1A-09 Thrombosis
Status: ⚪ | Class nos.: L 17; T 11,12
Core:
- Mechanism of thrombosis
- Fate of thrombus
- Clinical consequence of venous thrombosis, arterial and cardiac thrombosis
- DIC

### PATH-1A-10 Embolism and infarction
Status: ⚪ | Class nos.: L 18; T 11,12
Core:
- Definition of embolism
- Pulmonary embolism: source and consequence
- Systemic thromboembolism: source and consequence
- Air embolism, fat embolism, amniotic fluid embolism: source and consequence
- Infarct: definition, types, factors influencing the formation of infarct

### PATH-1A-11 Growth disturbance and adaptive change
Status: ⚪ | Class nos.: L 19; T 13; P 4
Core:
- Adaptive change
- Definitions and examples of atrophy, metaplasia, hypertrophy, hyperplasia
Additional:
- Mechanism of the adaptive changes

### PATH-1A-12 Neoplasia
Status: ⚪ | Class nos.: L 20,21,22,23; T 14,15; P 5,6,7
Core:
- Definition and characteristics of neoplasia
- Nomenclature
- Features of benign and malignant tumour
- Spread of tumour
- Genetic predisposition of cancer
- Example of proto-oncogene, cancer suppressor gene
- Precancerous conditions
Additional:
- Molecular basis of cancer
- Multiple step of carcinogenesis

### PATH-1A-13 Carcinogenesis
Status: ⚪ | Class nos.: L 24,25; T 16
Core:
- Chemical carcinogen: classification
- Tumour: initiation and promotion
- Microbiological carcinogen: name and the cancer associated with them
- Name of the radiant energy and the cancer associated with them
Additional:
- Mechanism of the carcinogenesis of the viruses and radiant energy, particularly of HPV, EBV and H. pylori

### PATH-1A-14 Tumor immunity and clinical aspects of neoplasia and laboratory diagnosis of tumor
Status: ⚪ | Class nos.: L 26; T 17
Core:
- Tumor antigen
- Antitumor mechanism
- Immune surveillance
- Cancer cachexia
- Paraneoplastic syndrome
- Grading and staging of tumor: basis and their use
- Laboratory diagnosis: role of FNAC, cytological examination, pap smear, frozen section and immunohistochemistry
Additional:
- Mechanism of immune surveillance
- Paraneoplastic syndrome
- Molecular diagnosis of cancer

### PATH-1A-15 Genetics
Status: ⚪ | Class nos.: L 27,28; T 18
Core:
- Basic definitions, mutation, type
- Classification of genetic disease
- Mendelian disorder: characteristics and examples
- Features of Down syndrome, Turner syndrome, Klinefelter syndrome and hermaphrodite
- Name of the tools for diagnosis of genetic disease: karyotype, FISH, PCR
Additional:
- Biochemical and molecular basis of single gene disorder, lysosomal storage disease
- Single gene disorder non-classical inheritance
- Indications of prenatal diagnosis

### PATH-1A-16 Immunopathology
Status: ⚪ | Class nos.: L 29,30; T 19
Core:
- Name of immune deficiency diseases
- Autoimmune diseases: name of the organ specific autoimmune diseases and the basic pathogenesis (name of the antibody)
- Name of the diagnostic tools

### PATH-1A-17 Infectious disease
Status: ⚪ | Class nos.: L 31; T 19
Core:
- Lesions produced by tuberculosis, leprosy and syphilis
- Name of the diagnostic tools

### PATH-1A-18 Nutritional disorders
Status: ⚪ | Class nos.: L 32,33; T 20
Core:
- Bone changes in deficiency states
- Features of vitamin A, vitamin B12 and folic acid deficiency
Additional:
- Iron metabolism
- Vitamin A and D metabolism
- Vitamin B12 and folic acid deficiency mechanism

### PATH-1A-19 Environmental diseases and hazards
Status: ⚪ | Class nos.: L 34,35; T 20
Core:
- Diseases associated with smoking, arsenicosis, radiation hazard

# BMDC Class Performance Card Items (cross-reference)

Card 1A — General Pathology
1. Introduction of pathology; histo-cytopathological sample collection, preservation, transport and processing
2. Cellular adaptations: definitions, feature and clinical significance; intracellular accumulation, calcification, cellular aging
3. Cell injury: definitions, injurious agents, types, reversible cell injury features and morphology; mechanism of hypoxic injury and free radicals
4. Irreversible cell injury: necrosis and apoptosis — features, example
5. Inflammation: definition, causes, cardinal signs, types; acute inflammation — cellular and vascular events; chemotaxis, phagocytosis
6. Chemical mediators, morphological patterns of acute inflammation, outcome, systemic effects of inflammation
7. Chronic inflammation: definition, cells, granulomatous inflammation — causes, examples, mechanism
8. Healing and repair: definition, types, mechanism, factors affecting wound healing, complications
9. Haemodynamics: oedema, effusions, electrolyte disorders
10. Hyperemia, congestion, haemorrhage, shock
11. Haemostasis, thrombosis, embolism, infarction
12. Neoplasia: definition, nomenclature, nature of tumor — benign, malignant, borderline malignancy, low malignant potential; incidence and predisposition
13. Features of malignancy: anaplasia, invasion, metastasis; molecular aspect of tumor — oncoprotein, oncogene, tumor suppressor gene, cellular and molecular hallmarks of cancer
14. Carcinogenesis: direct and indirect carcinogens; clinical aspects of cancer — cachexia, paraneoplastic syndrome, grading and staging
15. Tumor immunity, laboratory diagnosis of cancer

Card 1B — Haematolymphoid Pathology
1. Genetics: single gene disorders, chromosomal disorders, complex multigenic disorders; cytogenetic disorders — Down's, Turner's; mutation: definition, causes, types; diagnosis
2. Immunopathology: immunity, types; hypersensitivity, autoimmune disorders, immunodeficiency disorders; rejection of tissue transplantation
3. Nutritional disorders: PEM, obesity, vitamin and mineral deficiency; childhood tumor; environmental hazards — tobacco, alcohol; occupational hazards — arsenic, radiation; infectious disease — TB, leprosy, syphilis
4. Introduction and terminology: haematological sample collection, preservation, processing; constituents of blood and bone marrow; haematopoiesis; types of Hb and RBC indices; PBF, CBC
5. RBC disorder: anaemia, classification (aetiological, morphological); IDA and megaloblastic anemia — aetiopathogenesis, lab diagnosis
6. Haemolytic anaemia: extracorpuscular and intracorpuscular; thalassemia, sickle cell anaemia — aetiopathogenesis, lab diagnosis
7. Pancytopenia, aplastic anemia — aetiopathogenesis, lab diagnosis
8. WBC disorder: reactive proliferations — neutrophilia, leukocytosis, leukopenia, eosinophilia, lymphocytosis
9. Leukaemia and related disorders: leukaemia, leukaemoid reaction, subleukaemic leukaemia, myelodysplastic syndrome
10. Lymphoproliferative disorders: lymphadenitis, lymphoma — types, morphology of Hodgkin and non-Hodgkin; multiple myeloma
11. Myeloproliferative disorders: polycythemia, myelofibrosis
12. Haemorrhagic disorders: classification, ITP, haemophilia, DIC; screening tests (BT, CT, APTT, tourniquet test)
13. Blood grouping: types, blood products, screening tests, hazards of blood transfusion

Card 2A — Systemic Pathology
1. Blood vessels: atherosclerosis, vasculitis and tumors, lipid profile
2. Ischemic heart diseases, hypertensive heart diseases and cardiac enzymes
3. Congenital heart diseases, rheumatic fever, infective endocarditis (myocarditis, pericarditis, cardiomyopathy — types and causes)
4. Respiratory: congenital diseases, inflammatory diseases — TB, lung abscess, pneumonia
5. Respiratory: COPD — emphysema, chronic bronchitis, bronchial asthma, bronchiectasis, bronchogenic carcinoma, sputum examination
6. Urinary: congenital kidney diseases, clinical presentation of renal diseases, glomerular diseases — AGN, NS
7. Urinary: tubulo-interstitial diseases, pyelonephritis, renal calculi, renal function tests
8. Urinary: renal tumors and urinary bladder diseases — cystitis, bladder tumors
9. GIT: oral cavity, salivary gland — inflammation, classification of tumors (pleomorphic adenoma); oesophagus — precursor lesions, risk factors, tumors
10. Gastritis, peptic ulcer diseases, gastric carcinoma
11. Small and large intestine: congenital diseases, inflammatory bowel diseases, polyps and ulcers of GIT, tumors; acute appendicitis and tumour
12. Hepatobiliary: acute and chronic hepatitis — hepatitis B and C, viral markers, liver function tests
13. Hepatobiliary: liver cirrhosis, portal hypertension, hepatic failure and tumors
14. Gall bladder: calculi, aetiopathogenesis of cholecystitis, inflammation and tumors; pancreas — inflammation and tumors

Card 2B — Systemic Pathology
15. Male genital: testis — inflammations and tumors; semen analysis; prostate — NHP, tumors, PSA
16. Female genital: vaginal diseases — vaginitis, cyst; cervix — cervicitis, polyps, CIN, tumors, PAP smear
17. Female genital: corpus of uterus — DUB, adenomyosis, endometriosis, uterine tumors; placenta; ovary — cysts, tumors; pregnancy test
18. Breast: inflammatory and fibrocystic diseases, benign and malignant tumors — epidemiology, risk and prognostic factors; investigation protocols; IHC — ER, PR, HER-2
19. Endocrine: thyroid — hypo and hyperthyroidism; thyroiditis — Hashimoto's, Graves'; tumors — types, papillary carcinoma morphology, investigation protocols
20. Endocrine: diabetes mellitus, OGTT, Benedict's test
21. Eye and ENT: tumor, sinusitis, otitis media; CNS: inflammation — meningitis, brain abscess; brain tumors — glial tumors and others; criteria of brain tumors; CSF examination
22. Bones: inflammation — osteomyelitis; bone tumors classification — osteosarcoma; joints: rheumatoid arthritis; soft tissue tumors
23. Skin: common terms, inflammation, blistering diseases, pigmented skin lesions, premalignant and malignant conditions (SCC, BCC, malignant melanoma)
24. An outline of autopsy, techniques in histopathology, gross examination, tissue processing
25. Techniques in cytopathology: FNAC, Pap smear, fluid cytopathology, miscellaneous
26. Normal, increased and lower values of different haematopathological and chemical pathology investigations
