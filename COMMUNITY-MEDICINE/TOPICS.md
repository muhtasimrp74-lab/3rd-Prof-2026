# Community Medicine — BMDC Syllabus

Source (verified): BMDC Community Medicine PDF, exact file provided by you (https://www.bmdc.org.bd/docs/4-Community%20Medicine.pdf)
Note: this PDF is paginated 92–123 and its wording says "2nd Professional Examination".

Exam: 5 Nov — Written 100 (MCQ 20 + SAQ 70 + Formative 10), Structured oral 100, Practical 100 (OSPE 50 + RFST/survey/study tour report 30 + day visit report 20).
BMDC summative table: MCQ "10-20 questions to be answered", SAQ "12 questions to be answered". Pass mark: 60% in each of theory, oral, practical.

Status key: 🟢 Strong | 🟡 Needs revision | 🔴 Weak | ⚪ Not studied

BMDC hours shown as Lecture (L) / Tutorial (T). Totals: L 110 / T 160, integrated teaching 5 h, COME 30 days.
BMDC time schedule: Part 1 = units 1–3 (L 20 / T 30). Part 2 = units 4–15 (L 90 / T 130).
