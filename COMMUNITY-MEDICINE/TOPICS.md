Exam: 5 Nov — Written 100 (MCQ 20 [50% MTF + 50% SBA] + SAQ/SEQ 70 + Formative 10), Structured oral 100, Practical 100 (OSPE 50 + RFST/survey/study tour report 30 + day visit report 20).
Pass mark: 60% in each of theory, oral, practical.

# COME (Community Oriented Medical Education) — 30 days
Practical component (not written syllabus); relevant for Practical/Oral marks.
- RFST (Residential Field Site Training): 10 days — community health survey, MCH/FP services, UHC/PHC activities, upazila health complex, community clinic, FWC, NGO visit
- Day visits: at least 8 sites (e.g. DOTS corner, ORT corner, MCH clinic, FP clinic, UHC/community clinic, NGOs, industries, Civil Surgeon office, specialised institutes)
- Study tour: 10 days
