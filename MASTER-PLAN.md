# Master Plan (6 Oct – 15 Nov)

Exam order: Community Medicine (5 Nov) → Pathology (12 Nov) → Microbiology (15 Nov).
Unit IDs refer to each subject's `TOPICS.md` (PATH-, MICRO-, CM-).

Assumptions (edit if wrong):
- About 9 study hours/day. Hours below are per-day targets.
- Plan covers written exams only. Oral/practical dates are not in this plan.
- First pass = read + active recall once. Revision = recall from memory, past questions, weak list.

Weekly routine:
- Last day of each week: 30 min recall per subject on that week's units. Log gaps in WEAK-TOPICS.md.
- Every study day: write 1 answer (SAQ/SEQ) in the main subject. Log errors in MISTAKES.md.

## Risks (read once)

- Pathology systemic (13 units) is mostly first-pass during 6–11 Nov. Any slack before 5 Nov should go to PATH-2A first.
- Virology, Mycology and Clinical Microbiology are first-pass only during 6–11 Nov at 2 h/day. Tight but workable.
- CM-10 (CD and NCD) has the highest BMDC hours. Do not shortchange it.

## Rule

If a day is missed, drop the lowest-priority subject for that phase. Never move CM revision after 3 Nov.
